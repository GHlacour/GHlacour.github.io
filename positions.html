Post Doc positions

E-mail P.I. for details about applying for Dutch/European grants and other opportunities.
Applicants are expected to apply for their own funding, but the group can provide support and guidance in this process.
Options may include: Marie Curie, and VENI (NWO) grants or national grants from your home country.
It is recommended to start the application process well in advance of the desired start date, as it takes at least half a year between submitting a grant and receiving funding.
